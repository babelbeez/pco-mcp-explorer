Explore the Planning Center MCP server

A free, open-source tool for Planning Center developers. Sign in with your PCO account to see the live MCP tool manifest — every tool, parameter, and annotation, parsed into a readable, searchable table.

Connect to Planning Center

  1. We register this tool with Planning Center (OAuth dynamic client registration) and send you to Planning Center's secure sign-in.
  2. You choose exactly which scopes to grant on Planning Center's consent screen.
  3. You return here and browse the live tool manifest.

Everything runs in your browser. Your access token is kept only in this tab's session storage, is sent exclusively to Planning Center's servers (through a pass-through CORS proxy that stores nothing — see the README), and is revoked and discarded when you disconnect.
